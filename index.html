Manual

A SCROLL GUIDE — EVERYTHING THE CONSOLE CAN DO

Quick start

  1. Add images — upload, paste (⌘V / Ctrl+V) or drop them on the sheet.
  2. All images land at equal size on the white 16:9 canvas; adding more shrinks the rest automatically.
  3. Click an image to reveal its 8 resize handles plus the lock icon (top-right) and the rotate icon (below it).
  4. Drag a corner or edge — images sharing its row or column shrink or grow so the sheet stays full.
  5. Press the lock icon to freeze that image's proportions — it keeps its shape while everything around it changes.
  6. Press the rotate icon to turn the image 90° — press again to spin it through 180°, 270° and back to 0°.
  7. Tick ALLOW WHITE SPACE in the side panel to keep every image's original proportions — nothing is cropped or auto-resized, leftover paper stays white.
  8. Still in white space mode, press an image's crop icon (under the rotate icon) to free that one frame — drag its handles to change its proportions and the image crops to fill, while pinned neighbours keep their original shapes.
  9. Use GAP in the side panel to add breathing space between images, and RESET GRID to snap everything back to equal sizes.
  10. Drag an image body onto another to swap them. Export a print-ready PNG when done.

Lock icon

Click an image → padlock appears top-right. Press it to lock: that frame keeps its exact proportions through every other resize, add, swap or grid change. Locked frames that no longer fit are given white space around them instead of being squashed. Press again to unlock.

Rotate icon

Click an image → the rotate icon appears under the padlock. Press it to turn the image 90° clockwise. In auto-fit mode the frame stays put and the image is re-cropped to fill it; in white-space mode the frame swaps to the new proportions. Keep pressing to spin through 180°, 270° and back to square one.

Crop icon

Only offered while ALLOW WHITE SPACE is ticked — click an image and the crop icon appears under the rotate icon. Press it to free that frame: drag its handles to change its proportions and the image is cover-cropped to fill instead of being squeezed to fit. Cropped frames trade width with each other, or borrow it from the row when pinned neighbours are around — pinned images never lose their original shape. Press again to snap back to the uncropped natural ratio.

White space mode

The checkbox in the side panel. ON = every image keeps the proportions you gave it (its natural ratio) — the console will never crop or reshape it; leftover space on the paper stays white, and you control the sizes by dragging. Images with the crop icon switched on are exempt: they can be reshaped freely and are cropped to fill their frames. OFF = auto-fit layout crops images to fill their frames.
