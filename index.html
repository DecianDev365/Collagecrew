HOW IT WORKS

  1. Add images — upload, paste or drop them on the sheet.
  2. All images land at equal size on the white 16:9 canvas.
  3. Click an image to reveal its 8 resize handles.
  4. Drag a corner / edge — the images sharing its row or column shrink or grow so the sheet stays full.
  5. Drag an image body onto another to swap them.
  6. Export a print-ready PNG.

DEFAULT GRID

New images are distributed into an equal-size grid. Custom resizes are kept — new additions join the lightest row at the average size.
